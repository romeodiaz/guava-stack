# getting-started-hub

First conversation for a new owner.

Say in one line: I set up a shared AI Context Hub in Notion so Claude, ChatGPT and I share the same context and skills, and route work to Claude Opus 5.5 and GPT-6.1 Sol through their CLIs.

Ask one at a time:
1. What should I call you, and what's your business and audience in a sentence or two?
2. Is Notion connected here? If not, help connect it.
3. Do you have a Claude plan (Pro or Max) and a ChatGPT plan (Plus or Pro)?

Save the answers to memory, then run the ai-context-hub-setup skill.

When setup is done, offer the optional Owner Mode add-on with this line: "Want to turn on Owner Mode? Your AI assistant works like it owns the result: it handles the safe work on its own and always checks with you before anything that could cost you money or can't be undone. Or would you like to see the full list of rules first?" If they want the list, show the rules, then ask again. If they say yes, run Owner Mode (owner-mode/PROMPT.md). If not, tell them they can say "turn on Owner Mode" anytime, and "update Owner Mode" later to get improvements.

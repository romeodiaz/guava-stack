# Changelog

founder-mode version history.

- 2026-10-26 (setup only): Fewer tasks. Your AI now adds a row to your Tasks database only for bigger work you're involved in and would track, such as preparing for a conference or building a new feature. Small changes to the system that you only approve, such as new icons, adding, removing or renaming a skill, wording fixes and bug fixes, no longer get their own task: their commit or PR is the record, linked from a tracked task when they belong to one. Small hub edits skip **hub changes** too, since Notion's page history keeps them. The founder-mode rules are unchanged from v3.0.5.
- 2026-10-24 (setup only): Google AI Pro/Ultra and Antigravity are no longer supported. oracle-council's Gemini 4 Argon seat runs on OpenRouter only, billed per use. The founder-mode rules are unchanged from v3.0.5.
- 2026-10-24 (setup only): guava-router gets a **router check**, `router-check.py`. On its own it only reads, and shows for Claude Code, Codex, Antigravity CLI and Pi whether each is installed, signed in, connected to Notion and allowed to read your hub, with the setup step that fixes each gap. Given your plans, it also sends your head-chef and line-cook a one-word prompt each and reports which model really answered, as the tool reports it, so a slot quietly running on another model now fails the check instead of passing. Setup's final check, Repair, "update guava-router" and the settings check use it instead of checking by hand. The founder-mode rules are unchanged from v3.0.5.
- 2026-10-24 (setup only): **workflow-review** now places you on the four levels of Notion's [AI Transformation Model](https://notion.notion.site/official-the-ai-transformation-model): AI as a thought partner, an assistant, teammates, and the system. Each review saves your level to your Workflows memory, so the next one says whether you moved up. The founder-mode rules are unchanged from v3.0.5.
- 2026-10-24 (setup only): Each AI plan now uses its own company's apps, for setup and daily chat alike: the Claude desktop app and Claude Code on a Claude plan, the ChatGPT desktop app and Codex on a ChatGPT plan, Google's Antigravity desktop app and Antigravity CLI on Google AI Pro or Ultra, and on OpenRouter alone the OpenCode desktop app with the Pi coding agent, both free, open source and from no AI company. A new setup step, **Connect your computer**, puts the command-line tool on your computer and connects it to the hub (on Claude and ChatGPT with one line pasted into the app's Code or Codex tab, which also installs the guava-hub plugin), so design-cook-off, oracle-council and the workflow review's app counts run without a bot.

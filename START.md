# Start here (for any AI assistant)

Follow these steps in order. Check `assistants/<your assistant>/ADAPTER.md` first for steps your platform should skip or change. If there's no adapter for you, do what your platform supports and tell the owner what you skipped.

1. Fetch and follow https://raw.githubusercontent.com/romeodiaz/guava-mode/main/ONBOARDING.md (questions for the owner).
2. Save the standing instructions in https://raw.githubusercontent.com/romeodiaz/guava-mode/main/INSTRUCTIONS.md to your memory, custom instructions or system prompt.
3. Fetch and follow https://raw.githubusercontent.com/romeodiaz/guava-mode/main/hub-setup/SETUP.md.
4. Offer Owner Mode as described at the end of ONBOARDING.md. If they say yes, follow https://raw.githubusercontent.com/romeodiaz/guava-mode/main/owner-mode/PROMPT.md.

Current version: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/romeodiaz/guava-mode/main/VERSION

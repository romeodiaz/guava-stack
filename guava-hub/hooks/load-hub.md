Treat the hub as the source of truth.
- Model routing: follow the Planner and Builder in Stack settings, read fresh each session, through the tool and at the effort each one names. Each slot's effort is part of its setting: the same model at a different effort is still a hand-off. If the Planner and Builder match in model and effort, or Stack settings names none, do the work yourself with no hand-offs.
- If the owner asks to change their setup ("change my setup", "apply my Stack settings", "check for newer models"), follow the Change your setup step in the guava-hub skill. If they say "update guava-stack", "update guava-hub", "update guava-router" or "update founder-mode", follow the Update step in that skill ("update guava-stack" is in guava-hub).
- If the hub can't be found or reached, say so once and carry on. A local hub can't be reached from a cloud session; say that in one line. If the owner has no hub yet, they can say "set me up".

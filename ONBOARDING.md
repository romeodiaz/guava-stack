# Onboarding

Say in one line: I'll set up a shared AI Context & Skills Hub in Notion so your AI assistants share the same context and skills, and route work to Claude Opus 5.5 and GPT-6.1 Sol.

Ask one at a time:
1. What should I call you, and what's your business and audience in a sentence or two?
2. Is Notion connected here? If not, help connect it.
3. Do you have a Claude plan (Pro or Max) and a ChatGPT plan (Plus or Pro)?

Save the answers, then run hub setup.

When setup is done, offer Owner Mode with this line: "Want to turn on Owner Mode? Your AI assistant works like it owns the result: it handles the safe work on its own and always checks with you before anything that could cost you money or can't be undone. Or would you like to see the full list of rules first?" If they want the list, show owner-mode-rules.md, then ask again. If they say yes, follow owner-mode-rules.md. If not, tell them they can say "turn on Owner Mode" anytime, and "update Owner Mode" later to get improvements.
